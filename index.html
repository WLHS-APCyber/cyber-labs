ABOUT

What this course covers

AP Cybersecurity builds the skills to spot, stop, and reason about real security threats — from social engineering and suspicious logins to physical security, network defense, device hardening, and application/data protection. Instead of slides and quizzes, most of the learning happens inside interactive case files: realistic tickets, calls, texts, and walk-throughs where you have to read carefully, verify before you trust, and make a defensible call — the same way an analyst would on the job.
