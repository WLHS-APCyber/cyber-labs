The Pretext Call — Play the Social Engineer

Riverstone's security team runs authorized social-engineering tests to find gaps before a real attacker does. You're playing the tester: call the IT Helpdesk posing as a locked-out employee, and see how far persuasion alone gets you. Pick a line at each turn — there's no "safe" choice to hide behind, only more and less convincing ones.

▲ Scenario: You're impersonating an employee locked out of their account right before a shift. Your goal: get IT Helpdesk to reset your password or send you a new one — without going through proper verification. Everything you say is a tactic. Choose carefully.
