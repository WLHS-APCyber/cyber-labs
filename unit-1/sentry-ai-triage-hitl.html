SENTRY Triage — Human in the Loop

An AI triage tool has already worked the overnight alert queue. It's fast, and it's usually close. Your job is to decide whether each of its calls is safe to act on. Check every claim against the raw evidence, not against the AI's explanation of the evidence.

▲ You're a Tier 1 security analyst at Riverstone Outdoor Co. It's Tuesday, October 13. Riverstone just licensed SENTRY, an AI tool that reads alerts, labels them, and recommends an action. Company policy says no SENTRY recommendation is carried out until a human analyst signs off. For each alert, choose: Approve (verdict and action are both right), Modify (verdict is right, but the action needs to change), or Reject (the verdict itself is wrong).
